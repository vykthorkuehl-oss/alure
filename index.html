The first "bug" in history

By: Vykthor Gabryel Garcia Kuehl

On September 9, 1947,
engineers at Harvard University found a moth stuck inside the Mark II computer.
They taped the insect into the logbook with the note "first actual case of bug being found".
Since then, "bug" has become synonymous with errors in programs —
and "debug" is the act of hunting them down. 🐛

When video games break: The art of bugs

By: Vykthor Gabryel Garcia Kuehl

Ever fell through a map, encountered a missing texture box, or saw a character stretch into infinity?
Video game bugs turn physics engines and rendering pipelines upside down!
From classic T-poses to broken collision meshes, glitches show us just how fragile digital realities can be. 🎮💥
